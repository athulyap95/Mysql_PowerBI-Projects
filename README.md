<p>This project analyzes retail data to identify demand patterns, pricing effectiveness, and inventory efficiency using MySQL for comprehensive data integration and analysis. It supports the development of a multi-agent AI system by integrating data from stores, warehouses, suppliers, and customers to enable AI-driven demand forecasting, inventory tracking, and pricing optimization.</p>
<h4>Data Structure and Analysis:</h4>
<p>The database schema links three core tables—demand forecasting, inventory monitoring, and pricing optimization—through product ID and store ID, enabling multi-table joins for holistic analysis. Key performance indicators (KPIs) such as total sales, stock efficiency, promotion ROI, average lead time, and price elasticity are calculated using SQL queries to derive actionable insights. Findings reveal demand-inventory mismatches, significant promotion impacts during seasonal events, strong price sensitivity for certain products, and supply chain delays affecting availability.</p>
 <h4>Key Insights and Conclusion:</h4>
 <p>The analysis identifies products needing better inventory control, demonstrates how pricing and promotions influence demand, and highlights opportunities to align stock levels with expected sales. Actionable recommendations include optimizing restocking schedules, improving supplier coordination, and designing targeted pricing strategies to minimize holding costs and stockouts while maximizing retail performance.</p>
